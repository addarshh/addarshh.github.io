I work on dependable AI systems for conversational and tool-using workflows. My research spans reliable agents, trustworthy evaluation, voice AI, and efficient inference.

The common question is practical: how can an AI system remain useful when inputs are incomplete, tools fail, latency matters, or compute is limited? I approach that question through evaluation design, systems methods, and hardware-aware optimization.

Research

Four connected areas, organized around system reliability.

Reliable agents

Failure detection, state preservation, recovery, observability, and evaluation gates for tool-augmented language-model systems.

Trustworthy evaluation

Benchmarks and contrast sets that reveal where a metric or judge misses important aspects of model behavior.

Voice AI

Evaluation methods that preserve prosody, timing, and other information lost when speech is reduced to text.

Efficient inference

Hardware-aware model serving and on-device AI under latency, memory, power, and compute constraints.
