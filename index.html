Research threads

Research

Four threads, one systems question

How do we make capable AI dependable outside a clean benchmark?

01

Trustworthy evaluation

Benchmarks and contrast sets that expose where model behavior diverges from the outcome we intended to measure.

AI safety benchmarks, bias measurement, evaluation design

02

Reliable agents

Architectures that detect failure, recover state, and keep tool-using systems grounded over long workflows.

Agent orchestration, recovery, observability, evaluation gates

03

Voice AI

Evaluation methods that account for prosody, timing, and other information lost when speech is reduced to text.

Prosodic contrast sets, speech evaluation, semantic reliability

04

Efficient inference

Hardware-aware systems for responsive AI on constrained devices and latency-sensitive production paths.

On-device AI, quantization, caching, adaptive serving

Research practice

Measure, stress, repair

A practical loop for moving from promising model behavior to dependable systems.

Measure

Define the failure before optimizing the score.

Build evaluations that preserve the information the system actually receives, including speech, tool state, and hardware constraints.

Stress

Test the conditions a clean benchmark leaves out.

Probe ambiguity, latency, partial failures, distribution shifts, and resource limits before they become production surprises.

Repair

Design systems that can recover, not only predict.

Use observability, grounding, state preservation, and evaluation gates to make failure visible and correctable.
