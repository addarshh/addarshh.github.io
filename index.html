My name is Adarsh Agrawal. I am an Applied Scientist II at Amazon in Seattle, where I work on low-latency voice and conversational AI, retrieval-grounded agents, system reliability, and efficient model inference.

My research focuses on dependable AI under real operating constraints. I study reliable tool-using agents, operational memory, trustworthy evaluation, voice interfaces, and hardware-aware deployment. My work connects model behavior with the systems controls needed to make AI useful in practice.

Previously, I worked on generative AI optimization at Samsung Semiconductor, edge perception with Stony Brook University and U.S. Air Force Research Laboratory collaborators, and applied machine learning at World Wide Technology. I earned an M.S. in Computer Science from Stony Brook University and a B.Tech. in Computer Science from the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi.

I maintain a list of publications and technical reports under the Research tab. I am open to research collaborations in reliable agents, AI evaluation, voice AI, and efficient inference.

Research agenda

Four connected areas centered on dependable systems.

Reliable agents

Failure detection, operational memory, recovery, observability, and grounded tool use.

Trustworthy evaluation

Benchmarks and contrast sets that expose failure modes hidden by aggregate scores.

Voice AI

Evaluation and system design that preserve prosody, timing, and interaction context.

Efficient inference

Responsive AI under latency, memory, power, bandwidth, and compute constraints.
